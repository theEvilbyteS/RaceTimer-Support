❓ Frequently Asked Questions

  • Does the app require an account?
    No. All features work without signing in. Sign in with Apple is optional and is used only to back up your sessions to your personal iCloud.
  • How do I cancel my subscription?
    From the Account Menu → Subscription button → manage or cancel from native App Store screen.
  • My Apple Watch is not detecting my subscription.
    Open the RacerTime app on your iPhone first. This will sync your subscription status to the Watch automatically.
  • Does the Watch app work over a wetsuit?
    Yes, but you must disable Wrist Detection. Go to the Watch app on your iPhone → Passcode → turn off Wrist Detection. Without this step, the Watch may pause the session when it doesn't detect skin contact.

🔒 Privacy

RacerTime does not collect or transmit personal data to any server. Session data is stored locally on your device and optionally in your own iCloud account. We have no access to your data.
