Racing Timer

Judge Mode

Designed for race officials to manage race sequences and flag signals directly from the wrist. Independent color settings to distinguish roles.

⚓

GPS Buoy Guide

Configure custom race courses with 2–5 buoys. Navigate to each mark with real-time bearing and distance. Set your course anywhere on the water.
