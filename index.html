Why RacerTime

A premium toolkit for serious water sports sessions

From race countdowns to post-session analytics, RacerTime combines precision, clarity and robust offline behavior in one clean ecosystem.

⏱

Race Timer

Control your countdown directly from the Digital Crown. Reliable in wet conditions, with current speed, GPS distance and heart rate visible while racing.

🚩

Judge Mode

Built for race officials to run sequences and flag timing independently on the Apple Watch, with a distinct visual profile for judge workflows.

⚓

GPS Buoy Guide

Create custom race courses with 2 to 5 buoys and navigate precisely to each mark using real-time distance and bearing.

📊

Performance Analysis

Review sessions on iPhone with route maps, speed charts, trends and actionable performance metrics after every workout.

↺

Maneuver Detection

Automatic detection of tacks and gybes in sailing sports, including successful and failed maneuvers for deeper technique analysis.

Optional iCloud Backup

Sign in with Apple only if you want cloud backup. All features work without sign-in, and your sessions remain local unless you choose iCloud.
