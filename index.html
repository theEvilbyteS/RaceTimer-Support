❓ Frequently Asked Questions

  • Does the app require an account?
    No. All features work without signing in. Sign in with Apple is optional and is used only to back up your sessions to your personal iCloud.
  • How do I cancel my subscription?
    Open the iOS Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions → RaceTimer → Cancel Subscription.
  • My Apple Watch is not detecting my subscription.
    Open the RaceTimer app on your iPhone first. This will sync your subscription status to the Watch automatically.
  • Does the app work over a wetsuit?
    To use the Watch over a wetsuit, disable Wrist Detection in the Watch app → Passcode settings.

🔒 Privacy

RaceTimer does not collect or transmit personal data to any server. Session data is stored locally on your device and optionally in your own iCloud account. We have no access to your data.
